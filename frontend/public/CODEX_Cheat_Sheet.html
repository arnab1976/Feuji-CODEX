In one line: CODEX reads medical records (PDF/TIFF), codes every risk-adjusting condition to ICD-10 and CMS-HCC V28 with a cited rationale, and then decides for each code whether to auto-submit, send to a coder, flag for deletion or query the physician. The problem

  • Certified coders reach only 85–90% first-pass CMS acceptance, leaving a 10–15% error and rework gap.
  • Coding backlogs cost about $1.5M per plan per year; 40% of coder time goes on low-yield records.
  • Record volume exceeds what coders can process inside CMS deadline windows.
  • V28 (100% of the 2026 risk score) pays only for specific codes. Vague documentation now loses value, so specificity matters.

Who is the customer? B2B

B2B ✓ Risk-adjusted revenue, coding cost and RADV/DOJ exposure all sit with health plans and risk-bearing providers. They own the charts and the CMS submission. Inside Healthcare 360 it is at most B2B2C: the patient benefits from a better-documented visit but never sees or pays for CODEX.
B2C ✗ Individuals have no coding problem and no budget for one. CODEX processes PHI under HIPAA with BAAs and submits to CMS on a plan's behalf, which cannot work as a consumer product. Do not bundle it into the patient-side PRISM subscription.

Compliance guardrails (lead with these)

  • Two-way coding: add and delete. DOJ treats one-directional chart review as an enforcement theory (Aetna, $117.7M, Mar 2026).
  • Encounter-linked only: CMS excludes unlinked chart-review diagnoses from CY2027 risk scores.
  • Timely, non-leading queries: within 24 h, multiple choice, "not assessed" option, no payment language (Kaiser, $556M, Jan 2026, involved delayed addenda).
  • Eligibility before confidence: face-to-face, acceptable provider, no lab-only or inferred severity.
  • MEAT + cited source per code; HIPAA BAA with the LLM provider; PHI in the customer VPC.

Fix in SA-07 before sharing: "RAPS/EDPS auto-submit" → encounter-linked EDS; Addendum AI "improve HCC capture" → compliant clinical query; add deletes to the KPIs.

Objection → answer

  • "AI upcoding risk?" Two-way, cited, gated, auditable. We sell accuracy, not code volume.
  • "Our coders will resist." They keep the judgement calls; CODEX removes the low-yield 40%.
